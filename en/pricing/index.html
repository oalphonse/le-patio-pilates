Good to know

Your questions about our options.

Everything you need to know before you choose.

Can I pause my membership?

Yes, you can freeze it for 1 month a year. Just ask at least 1 week before the month in question; a month that has already started cannot be frozen.

What happens if I cancel my membership?

A fee of 50% of one monthly payment applies: €75 (5 classes) or €140 (10 classes).

How do referrals work?
