Frequently asked questions

I've never done Reformer. Is it for me?

Yes. Classes are open to all levels and limited to six people: your instructor guides you step by step and adjusts the springs to your body.

What should I bring to my first class?

Comfortable clothes and grip socks, which are required on the Reformer. If you forget them, we sell them at the studio. Changing rooms and showers are available.

How late can I cancel a class?

[Cancellation deadline to be confirmed.] You can cancel in seconds via bsport or the app.

Pregnant, injured or recovering: can I take part?

Talk to your doctor first, then let us know before the class. Exercises can be adapted.

How do I get to the studio and where can I park?

There are six secure spaces on site and a public car park right next door. Your access codes are sent to you when you book.

I live in or near Chantilly. Is it far?

No. The studio is at 43 avenue Félix Louat in Senlis, about 15 minutes by car from Chantilly, with six parking spaces on site and a public car park right next door. It is also easy to reach from Chamant, Courteuil or Aumont-en-Halatte.
